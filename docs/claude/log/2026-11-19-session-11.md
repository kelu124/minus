## Part 4 — ADC and chain noise / ENOB
- Owner: on the basis of the gain, calculate the actual ENOB and the noise performance of the ADC and chain.
- No firmware change. Internal ADC inputs found in DS70005592 Table 18-2: AD1AN13 temp diode, AD1AN14 15/16 VDD, AD1AN15 band gap
  0.8 V (ADC2 same → codes 30/31). `bench/2026-11-19-noise/capture.sh` → 51 records; `analyze_noise.py` → summaries + plots;
  in-band table `band_1p5-5MHz.txt`. Full write-up in `bench/2026-11-19-noise/README.md`.
- Results: ADC alone σ 0.82–1.0 LSB = 10.2–10.5 noise-limited bits (datasheet 10.5); AVDD 3.32 V from the band gap.
  Every external input carries a period-4 fixed pattern (one sample in 4 ≈ 23 LSB low, 10.5 LSB rms, gain-independent, phase
  random per capture) — not on internal inputs. Chain (pattern removed) 3.7 → 15.1 LSB from ×1 to ×134 = 8.3 → 6.3 bits full
  band; in 1.5–5 MHz 0.45 → 4.2 LSB = 11.4 → 8.2 bits. Front end: 85 µV rms at OA1 input; at 42.5 dB ≈ 13 nV/√Hz at OA1 in
  ≈ 0.85 nV/√Hz at the LNA input (24 dB assumed) ≈ 50 Ω thermal. Interference: 2.72 MHz (in band) + 0.49 MHz, input-side.
  LNA tap reads 26 mV rms of aliased wideband noise.
