## Q1 — what must be fixed around the PIC op-amps so a small echo is amplified?
Answer (ranked): (1) single-supply bias — RX node is ground-referenced and sits at +0.6 V (D7 + R28/R36 to the
2.5 V FE-B/C inputs) → OA1 ×11 saturates (3.27 V measured 2026-10-28) and the negative half is lost; AC-couple +
Vmid bias + clamp around Vmid; D7's −0.6 V likely exceeds the −0.3 V pin abs max. (2) Series cap in R47/R49
(DC gain 1; ±3 mV offset × 121 = ±0.36 V). (3) Interstage C + re-bias OA1OUT → OA2IN+ (owner's point; ~100 kHz HP
useful). (4) Clean Vmid (OA3 as buffer). (5) Drop the digipot (1 MHz cap, 120 pF on IN−). (6) IN− layout, TMS stub.
(7) Output routing to the ADC node + anti-alias 6–8 MHz + low-Z drive. (8) Bench-only: noise, overload recovery,
AVDD decoupling.

## Q2 — easy fixes for ×1/×2/×5/×10 instead of the digipot
GPIO-switched legs behind **one shared DC-block cap** (Rf 10 k; legs 10 k / 2.49 k / 1.11 k; all Hi-Z = ×1). Per-leg
caps would drift and glitch when switched; a shared cap keeps node X at ~0 V → stepped TGC is clean.

## Q3 — 74HC4051?
Bandwidth (~100–200 MHz) is not the issue; Ron 100–200 Ω at 3.3 V is. Put it at the ground end of the legs (switch
sees ~0 V, Ron constant), raise Rf to 20 k (legs 20 k / 4.99 k / 2.1 k → ×2/×4.9/×10). Bonus: 8 gains from 3 GPIOs.
Figures from memory — datasheet not downloaded.

## Q4 — use the 4 spare channels for OA1 (×10 × ×10)? dB?
One 4051 has one common → only one leg on → only one stage gains (max ×10). 4052 (paired) or 4053 / two 4051s
needed. ×100 = 40 dB. Recommendation: OA1 fixed ~×10 (sets noise), 8 OA2 steps in 3 dB increments.

## Q5 — limit of the 100 MHz GBW per stage
≈ ×10 per stage practical (single stage −1 dB at 5 MHz: ×10; 2 stages: ×7 each = 34 dB at −1 dB, ×13 = 44 dB at
−3 dB; 3 stages: 45 / 60 dB). HPEN mandatory (LP: 50 MHz GBW, 10 V/µs → 0.6 Vpp at 5 MHz).

## Q6/Q7 — 20 dB amplifiers below 1 €; look at the BGA28xx MMIC approach
Options: MMIC gain block, discrete BJT (2SC3356/BFR92), OPA356 (~$1–1.4). Owner asked to study the MMIC.
Downloaded (NXP site blocks scripted downloads; mirrors used): BGA2869 (hwlab.fit.cvut.cz) and BGA2817
(cdn.eicom.ru) → `pdfs/datasheets/` with `.md`. Result: **BGA2817 = 24 dB, 3.3 V/20 mA, NF 3.9 dB, OP1dB +6 dBm,
LCSC C2654187** is the pick; BGA2869 = 31 dB, 5 V. Both characterised only ≥ 250 MHz → 3–5 MHz behaviour must be
measured. Proposed circuit (10 nF couplers, BAV99 clamp, 51 Ω termination to decoupled Vmid that also biases OA1
IN+), gain budget 44–64 dB, noise ≈ 7 LSB rms at 64 dB → **memory 0013**.
