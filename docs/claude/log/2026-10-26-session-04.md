## Programming spec check (DS70005583)
- ICSP = 2-wire bit-bang: MCLR low → 0x8A12C2B2 LSB-first on PGD with 32 PGC clocks →
  MCLR high → 2×34 clocks; then 2-bit commands (CMDEXEC/CMDRD/CMDSEQWR/CMDSEQRD) + 32-bit
  shifts. Timing mins 20 ns setup / 1 ns hold. **RP can do it** from GPIO/PIO. LVP is the
  only mode (no HV Vpp). VIL max on MCLR/PGx = 0.2·VDD = 0.66 V.

## Flaws found
1. R68‖R69 = 50 Ω short between SPI_MOSI and SPI_MISO → remove.
2. R53 4k7 vs R5 10k: GPIO24 low only pulls MCLR to ~1.05 V (> 0.66 V) → R53 = 0 Ω; cut JP1
   (C31) for ICSP.
3. JTAG default-on on an erased PIC sits on the SPI pins → `JTAGEN = OFF` in config.
4. ICSP/SWD sharing on J1 — keep RP GPIO21/22/24 Hi-Z unless programming.
5. 5 V WS2812/SK6812 driven at 3.3 V (VIH 3.5 V) — marginal.
